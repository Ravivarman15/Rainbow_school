# Rainbow Matriculation School — Media Inventory & Visual Asset Strategy

## 1. Public Media Audit Results
- **Official High-Resolution Campus Photos**: Not present in verified public domains specifically for the Orathi campus.
- **Third-Party / Mixed Photos**: Public search engines occasionally present photos from similarly named schools in Tiruppur, Coimbatore, or Madurai. **Per strict prompt safety directives, these photos are NEVER mixed into this website.**
- **Official Brand Mark / Crest**: No digital vector logo exists in public registries.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Creative Strategy

To maintain uncompromising design excellence while preserving 100% authenticity, the web application employs a modern visual identity system:

### A. Dynamic Abstract Education Visuals & Illustrations
- Clean geometric illustrations representing:
  - Early Childhood Play & Learning (Pre-Primary / Kindergarten)
  - Foundational Literacy & Mathematics
  - Nature, Growth & Outdoor Play
  - Science Discovery & Creative Arts
- Handcrafted SVG illustrations and micro-gradient canvases representing school life with high visual refinement.

### B. Institutional Typographic Identity
- Clean, authoritative wordmark:
  - `RAINBOW` in crisp modern bold sans-serif with subtle chromatic underline accent.
  - `MATRICULATION SCHOOL • ORATHI` in clean tracked small-caps.
  - Subtitle: *Affiliated to TN Matriculation System | Est. 2009*.

### C. Color Palette
- **Deep Academic Blue / Slate (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#312E81`)**: Evokes discipline, institutional pedigree, trust, and structure.
- **Emerald Growth Green (`#059669`, `#10B981`)**: Evokes rural Tamil Nadu greenery, vitality, and child development.
- **Subtle Rainbow Prismatic Accents (`#6366F1`, `#EC4899`, `#F59E0B`, `#06B6D4`)**: Applied gently in micro-glows, badges, and active tab indicators rather than childish multi-colored backgrounds.
- **Warm Canvas Neutral (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F1F5F9`)**: Clean whitespace for maximum readability.

### D. Administrator Photo Upload Slots
- Dedicated, beautifully styled photo slots across the Campus and Gallery sections clearly labelled with an elegant upload indicator badge:
  - `[ Official Campus Photography Slot — School Admin Managed ]`
- Allows the school administrators or webmaster to easily drop in real photographs when available.
